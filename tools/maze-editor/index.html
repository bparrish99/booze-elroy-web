Booze Elroy

maze editor · 28×31 · mirrored · standard pen

Paint

A new maze is solid wall: carve paths. Left paints; right paints wall back (with the Wall tool: dots). Shift+drag fills a rectangle. Everything mirrors left↔right; the pen and the tiles round it (and under Pac-Man) are fixed.
